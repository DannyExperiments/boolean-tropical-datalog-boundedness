# Q1078 literature assessment

Checked 2026-11-27. High confidence in the question and its semiring conventions. Moderate confidence in the bounded finding that no checked paper gives the three-rule separation.

- **Source and model.** Fan, Koutris and Roy, *Circuits and Formulas for Datalog over Semirings*, PODS 2025, Article 93, Definition 4.1 and §4 after Proposition 4.8 (p. 93:10). Boundedness is a uniform iteration count for the target. The authors ask whether Boolean boundedness can separate from boundedness over an absorptive semiring, explicitly mentioning tropical. Their PODS slides pose the same question on slide 16. Section 2.4 and Proposition 2.4 take the product over leaf occurrences and characterize the tight-tree semantics. Repeating an extensional fact on different branches is allowed. The author PDF carries the proceedings DOI and article pagination. The publisher full-text fetch was blocked, so the statement and exact page were verified in the author PDF and talk slides. [Author PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.08914v1#page=10) · [PODS slides](https://austen-z-fan.github.io/assets/slides/PODS_2025_Datalog_Circuit.pdf#page=16) · [Proceedings DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3725230)
- **Nearby transfers.** Theorem 4.5 gives a finite-expansion criterion. Corollary 4.7 gives equivalence with Boolean boundedness for absorptive, multiplicatively idempotent semirings. In the nonnegative tropical semiring, multiplication is numeric addition and need not be idempotent. Ordinary unguarded reachability is itself Boolean-unbounded. The three-rule construction gives a Boolean shortcut for all inputs, with duplicate leaf occurrences making the shortcut relatively expensive under tropical weights. [Source §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.08914v1#S4)
- **Size-dependent convergence.** Abo Khamis et al.'s convergence theorems, JACM 2024, concern finite convergence and estimates in parameters of grounded or input-dependent systems. Zhao et al., PODS 2024, give faster grounding and fixpoint evaluation. Frisk et al., *Optimal Convergence of Iterative Methods for Datalog*, arXiv:2610.04062v1, October 2026, give sharp order bounds in the number of ground intensional tuples for linear and general programs over stable semirings (Theorems 1.1, 1.2). None of those statements compares uniform bounds over all databases of one already Boolean-bounded program. [Abo Khamis et al.](https://repositum.tuwien.at/handle/20.500.12708/209322) · [Zhao et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12436) · [Frisk et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04062v1#S1)
- **Circuit companion.** The source §3 cites the Karchmer–Wigderson lower bound. Its Proposition 3.6 transfers Boolean-support lower bounds to positive semirings, and §5 discusses path doubling. The companion isolates a target with Boolean provenance equal to one input while retaining an integer threshold that encodes connectivity. The reduction uses the 0 and 1 tests of each min-plus gate; it applies with nonnegative integer constants or infinity. The square-logarithmic depth result is for that target, using the classical bounds. Internal Boolean reachability can be unbounded. This does not classify possible depths in general. [Source §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.08914v1#S3) · [Source §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.08914v1#S5) · [Karchmer–Wigderson](https://doi.org/10.1137/0403021)
